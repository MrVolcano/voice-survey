Voice Feedback prototypes

Two versions of the same post-training feedback survey, read aloud and answered by voice or by tapping on screen

Both prototypes ask the same core questions from the real training feedback survey. They differ only in how they handle the two question types that suit voice input worst: a long list of yes/no outcome questions, and a long checklist of new abilities. Try both to compare how they feel to sit through.

Full detail

Every outcome (confidence, independence, isolation, and so on) and every new ability from the real survey is asked about individually. Most faithful to the original survey, but noticeably longer - around 30 questions in total.

Try the full-detail version

Open-ended

The same outcomes and new abilities are instead covered by two open questions ("what's changed for you?" / "what can you do now?"), keeping the whole survey to around 10 questions.

Try the open-ended version
Where does the data go? Each response is saved to this browser's local storage on this device - nothing is sent anywhere by default. A survey summary screen also offers a "Download my answers" button (a CSV file). If a Power Automate webhook URL has been configured in survey-engine.js, responses are also posted into an Excel table on the organisation's Microsoft 365 tenant. See README.md for how to set that up.
